Inspiration

these are a few creatives that inspire me

Sam Youkilis

Work by Sam Youkilis


Sam Youkilis is an American photographer based in Italy.

His work focuses on everyday life, capturing people, landscapes, traditions, and small moments that can easily go unnoticed.

I am inspired by how he uses contrast, color, composition, and natural light to make ordinary moments hold my attention. His work reminds me to slow down and notice the people and places around me that can become easy to overlook when they are part of a routine.

Otl Aicher

Image by Sven Simon


Otl Aicher was a German graphic designer best known for his work for the 1972 Munich Olympics and for developing the pictogram system that influences many of the symbols we see in public spaces today. I admire his view of graphic design as a form of public infrastructure because it shows how design can have a practical purpose and shape how people navigate and understand the world around them.

Jasmine Dowling

Work by Jasmine Dowling


Jasmine Dowling is a digital designer whose work combines photography, illustration, collage, and stop motion animation.

I am inspired by how her work brings physical material into a space that is usually very digital. The texture of paper, paint, thread, flowers, and other mediums she utilizes make her work feel tactile and give it a sense of nostalgia now in the digital age.

September 9, 2026

While creating this page, I learned how important it is to organize my code in a way that makes it easy to digest and skim through. I realized that placing opening and closing tags on their own lines and indenting the content inside made it much easier to see which elements belong together. This became especially useful as I added more content to the page and my HTML became longer and I wanted to moe things around.

I organized the names of each creative and their images inside a tags so that both can take visitors to their respective website on another tab. I also added attribution underneath each image using a paragraph tag and used CSS to make the font size extra small. This made me wonder if there is a more specific HTML tag I could use for attribution like a caption tag as I learn more HTML. I also want to eventually create more hierarchy between attribution and my descriptions rather than relying on header tags with bold text. I am not completely satisfied with the current state of the page because of its simplicity, but I am excited to build on it. WIth more time and CSS knowledge, I would like to center the images, improve the spacing and hierarchy, and experiment with the layout to make the page reflect my own design style.
