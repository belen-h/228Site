Process Reflection

Before applying Flexbox, I had to figure out how I was going to group everything in the HTML. I wrote down which div would be the flex-container/parent and which would be the flex-items/children. I decided that the content div would be the flex container, while the lyrics and album information would be the flex items.

Once I applied display: flex, the two sections were no longer stacked on top of each other but appeared side by side. I added temporary background colors to the containers so I could see what was changing as I experimented with their percentage widths. This helped me visualize how the content, padding, and margins affected the space each column occupied. Once I got the widths close to the reference image, I started adjusting the margins and padding. I ended up using fairly small values because the layout didn't need much additional spacing.

One problem I encountered was that my font wasn't loading correctly. After looking through my HTML, I realized the file path to the font was incorrect. Once I fixed it, the font loaded perfectly!

Something I still find a little confusing is margin and padding. Every exercise makes me feel like I’ve finally mastered the difference, but I still find myself having to refresh my memory when applying them to a new layout.
